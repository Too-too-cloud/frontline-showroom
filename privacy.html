Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 27, 2026

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide to us via email inquiries, such as your name, contact email address, and any details regarding your furniture interests. We do not use cookies or automated tracking scripts on this website.

Any information shared is used strictly to respond to your requests, provide details on our Bespoke Seating and Executive Workspace collections, or to schedule private viewings.

We do not sell, rent, or trade email lists or personal data with other companies or businesses for marketing purposes. Your privacy is paramount to our brand.

4. Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this policy or your data, please reach out to us at: info@frontlinefurnishers.org
